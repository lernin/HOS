Quick Help

Handy controls:

  • Arrows or J/K/L/I – move node selection
  • Double-click node – rename (Enter=save, Esc=cancel)
  • Drag node – reparent; drag to Trash=delete
  • Mix (M) – shuffle tree; Shift+M or right-click Mix includes WordBank
  • Word Dock – Click=select, Shift+Click=multi-select; drag to tree/Trash
  • Ctrl+A over Dock – toggle select all chips
  • F=Fit • W=Dock • A=Input • U=Undo • P=PNG export
  • Settings – adjust spacing, detectors
  • Esc – close modal/editor
