# **Presidia Connection Code Model**

**(Identity · Context · Exposure · Interaction)**

## **1. Core idea (one sentence)**

Every connection is a **two-person handshake** where the **giver** (always a Presidia member) optionally exposes *specific contexts* (schools / sections) so the **receiver** can say *where* they know them—or just connect socially.

## **2. Two roles (always)**

  - **Giver**: shows the QR / code
      
      - Always a Presidia member
      - Chooses what (if anything) to expose
  - **Receiver**: scans the code
      
      - May or may not already be a member
      - Decides how they know the giver (from exposed options)

There is **no connection without intent**. Nothing is assumed.

## **3. Base symbols (glossary)**

### **Person / membership**

  - **M** – Presidia member
  - *(absence of M)* – not yet a member

### **Facility / place**

  - **C** – student presence at a place
  - **1, 2, 3…** – indexed places (elementary, academy, etc.)  
      
     Numbers are local labels, not global IDs.  
      
      

### **Enrollment (school-level)**

  - **e** – enrollment at a school that is *not fully authenticated*
  - **E** – enrollment at a *fully authenticated* school  
      
     If **E** exists, section membership is assumed.  
      
      

### **Sections (inside a place)**

  - **lowercase letter (a, b, c)****  
    ** → physically in that section, but **no Presidia section membership yet**
  - **CAPITAL letter (A, B, C)****  
    ** → active **section membership** exists in Presidia

## **4. Exposure (privacy control)**

### **Prime mark ( ′ )**

A **prime** means: *“I am choosing to expose this.”*

  - **No primes at all****  
    ** → only identity is exposed (name-level connection)
  - **Prime on a place or section****  
    ** → receiver may select that context as “where we know each other”

### **Examples**

  - C1′  
     → “I’m willing to reveal that I’m at place 1.”
  - C1aB′  
     → “I’m at place 1; I’m exposing section **B** only.”
  - CE2′AB  
     → “I’m enrolled at authenticated place 2 and exposing it.”

**Rule:****  
** Only **primed contexts** can auto-match. Everything else requires later confirmation.

## **5. Reading a full code (giver → receiver)**

### **Example (canonical)**

C1 → M C1 a B | C E2 A B

  

### **Plain English**

  - **Giver**:
      
      - Goes to **place 1**
      - In sections **a** (no system link) and **B** (linked)
  - **Receiver**:
      
      - Is a Presidia member
      - Also at **place 1**, section **B**
      - Additionally enrolled (**E**) at **place 2** with sections **A** and **B**

The UI can now ask:

“Where do you know each other?”  
 and only show **exposed overlaps**.

## **6. Common real-world cases**

### **Case 1: Typical kid (elementary + academy)**

C1 → M C1 B | C E2 A

  

  - Same elementary class (B)
  - Enrolled at an academy (2), section A

### **Case 2: Same school, different classes**

C1 → M C1 A | C1 B

  

  - Same school, different sections
  - Handshake can **create awareness** without forcing closeness

### **Case 3: Academy only (authenticated)**

C2 → M C E2

  

  - Both enrolled at the same verified academy
  - No section choice needed

### **Case 4: Early “nebula” school (teacher found Presidia)**

C1 → M C1 a B

  

  - School exists socially
  - Section **B** is live in Presidia
  - Enrollment may come later

### **Case 5: Social-only connection**

→ M

  

  - No places exposed
  - Just: “I know this person”

## **7. Rules that keep this sane**

1.  **First person is always the giver****  
    ** → no need to encode giver/receiver roles
2.  **No enrollment without a school****  
    ** → enrollment is evidence of institutional recognition
3.  **No forced intimacy****  
    ** → connection ≠ relationship depth
4.  **Exposure is intentional****  
    ** → nothing leaks by default
5.  **Schools mature upward****  
    ** → nominated → confirmed → addressed → authenticated  
     (codes reflect reality, not authority fantasies)

## **8. Why this works**

  - Handles messy real life (partial adoption, uneven authority)
  - Respects privacy
  - Scales from **two kids** to **UBC**
  - Feeds directly into:
      
      - Dunbar layers (5 / 15 / 50 / 150)
      - Augmented Communication Bridge
      - Social + learning graphs
