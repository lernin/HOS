# Growth Strategy 'try before buy'  

# **Report: Growth Strategy**

## **“Experience Before Signup”**

### **Overview**

Procedia’s primary growth mechanism is not traditional onboarding or marketing. Instead, it relies on immediate, in-person experiential demonstration. The product spreads through short, high-impact live interactions before any account creation occurs.

This model prioritizes emotional engagement and perceived value before requesting user commitment.

### **Core Principle**

**Experience first. Infrastructure second.**

Users should:

1.  Try the system instantly.  
      
2.  Feel its intelligence and responsiveness.  
      
3.  See practical value.  
      
4.  Then choose to connect via QR code.  
      

The QR code is not the entry point.  
 The experience is.

### **Use Case Flow**

**Step 1: Live Demonstration**

  - Existing user hands phone to parent/student.  
      
  - Guest interacts immediately (no login barrier).  
      
  - AI responds in real time.  
      
  - User experiences correction, engagement, and clarity.  
      

**Step 2: Emotional Buy-In**

  - User feels: “This is useful.”  
      
  - Perceived intelligence builds trust.  
      
  - No pressure, no sales framing.  
      

**Step 3: Seamless Connection**

  - QR code offered.  
      
  - Quick connect to home/section/community.  
      
  - Account creation happens after trust is established.  
      

### **Design Implications**

  - Guest Mode must be instant and frictionless.  
      
  - Demo interactions must be visually impressive and responsive.  
      
  - AI feedback must feel intelligent within 30–60 seconds.  
      
  - QR connection must be simple and fast.  
      
  - No heavy setup before first interaction.  
      

### **Strategic Rationale**

Most educational platforms:

  - Lead with signup forms.  
      
  - Require commitment before value.  
      
  - Rely on marketing persuasion.  
      

Procedia:

  - Demonstrates value first.  
      
  - Builds trust through interaction.  
      
  - Converts through lived experience.  
      

This increases:

  - Organic word-of-mouth growth.  
      
  - Community trust.  
      
  - Parent comfort.  
      
  - Perceived innovation.  
      

### **Positioning**

This is not a “download and explore” product.

It is a:

“Let me show you something” product.

Growth occurs through demo-based virality rather than advertisement-based acquisition.
