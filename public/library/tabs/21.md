## **ð¹ Peristonym — Situational Context**

A **Peristonym** is a network of co-present elements within a situation.

  - Composed primarily of **Phenonyms**
  - Represents **what exists together**

**Example**

doctor → hospital, patient, scalpel, clinic

  

## **ð¹ Dromonym — Temporal Sequence**

A **Dromonym** is an ordered progression of elements across time.

  - Composed of **Phenonyms in sequence**
  - Represents **what happens over time**

**Example**

order → eat → pay

  

## **ð¹ Logonym — Language Context**

A **Logonym** is a set of elements that co-occur in language.

  - Includes **Phenonyms + Doxonyms**
  - Represents **how things are expressed**

**Example**

doctor → patient, examined, said, allegedly

  

## **ð¹ Syndonym — Expression Unit**

A **Syndonym** is a fixed or conventional expression.

  - Multi-word or patterned unit
  - Functions as a stable linguistic form

**Example**

by and large

kick the bucket

  - **Peristonym** = situational (observable network)
  - **Dromonym** = constrained (ordered sequence)
