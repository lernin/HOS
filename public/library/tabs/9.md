# **ð§  PART 2 — RULES & PEDAGOGICAL MODEL (MASTER)**

### **1. Two time scales**

**A) Micro-lesson (within one sitting) uses rungs:****  
** **introduction | reproduction | comprehension | recognition | production | application | transmission****  
** **Rungs describe** ***interaction type*****, not long-term mastery.**

**B) Spaced repetition (across time) decides** ***when*** **concepts return.****  
** **Spaced repetition and micro-lessons connect through** **concept\_mastery****.**

### **2. Evidence model (the bridge)**

**Each interaction can emit concept evidence:**

  - **\*\*rung\*\* \*\*= what kind of interaction happened\*\***
  - **\*\*result\*\* \*\*=\*\* \*\*correct | error\*\*** 
  - **\*\*strength\*\* \*\*= 0–5 (how strong the signal is)\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***

### **3. Rungs (what they mean)**

  - **\*\*Introduction\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Exposure to the idea or form.\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Rule: counts as a seed (low strength), not proof.\*\***
  - **\*\*Reproduction\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Copy/imitate with a model present.\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Rule: scaffold; weak evidence.\*\***
  - **\*\*Comprehension\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Meaning/rule understanding shown through meaning tasks (explain, choose reasoning, paraphrase).\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Rule: medium evidence when correct.\*\***
  - **\*\*Recognition\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Discriminate correctness with cues (MCQ, T/F, verification).\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Rule: weaker evidence because guessing exists.\*\***
  - **\*\*Production\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Generate from memory (spelling, free response, solve).\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Rule: strong evidence when correct.\*\***
  - **\*\*Application\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Use correctly in authentic context (writing, speaking, novel problems).\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Rule: very strong evidence.\*\***
  - **\*\*Transmission\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Teach/explain to another person.\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\* \*\*Rule: strongest evidence.\*\***

### **4. Default strength mapping (tunable)**

  - **\*\*Introduction:\*\* \*\*correct @ 0–1\*\***
  - **\*\*Reproduction:\*\* \*\*correct @ 1\*\***
  - **\*\*Recognition:\*\* \*\*correct @ 1–2\*\* \*\*(lower if T/F),\*\* \*\*error @ 2–3\*\***
  - **\*\*Comprehension:\*\* \*\*correct @ 2–3\*\*\*\*,\*\* \*\*error @ 2–3\*\***
  - **\*\*Production:\*\* \*\*correct @ 3–4\*\*\*\*,\*\* \*\*error @ 3–4\*\***
  - **\*\*Application/Transmission:\*\* \*\*correct @ 5\*\*\*\*,\*\* \*\*error @ 5\*\***
  - **\*\*Inferred (implicit signals): usually\*\* \*\*inferred @ 1\*\* \*\*(weak)\*\***

  

### **5. Session flow (within a micro-lesson)**

  - **\*\*Struggle → drop rung\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Success → probe upward (at most one rung above current stable level)\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*End on a win\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*No repeated failure loops at the same rung\*\***

## **4. How the system uses difficulty signals**

**The system already knows:**

  - **\*\*which rung was attempted\*\***
  - **\*\*whether the response was correct\*\***
  - **\*\*current stability\*\***
  - **\*\*which lane is due\*\***

**The learner’s signal nudges behavior:**

  - **\*\*easy\*\***  
      - **\*\*allow an upward probe (if permitted)\*\***
      - **\*\*slightly widen spacing\*\***
  - **\*\*ok\*\***  
      - **\*\*continue normally\*\***
  - **\*\*hard\*\***  
      - **\*\*avoid upward probe\*\***
      - **\*\*tighten spacing\*\***
      - **\*\*drop rungs sooner if needed\*\***

**No punishment.****  
****No forced escalation.****  
****No cognitive overhead.**

## **5. The second time scale: spaced repetition (across days / weeks / months)**

  - **\*\*Controls when a concept returns\*\***
  - **\*\*Does not dictate how a lesson behaves\*\***
  - **\*\*Scheduling and pedagogy are independent systems\*\***

**These systems connect through concept\_mastery.**

## **6. Rungs vs. stability (critical distinction)**

  - **\*\*Rung = the kind of task\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\*\*\*(introduction, reproduction, comprehension, recognition, production, application, transmission)\*\***
  - **\*\*Stability = how reliable performance is over time\*\***

**Fluency is not a rung.****  
****Fluency = stability at a rung, proven by success + time.**

  

### **8. Success accumulation (not brittle streaks)**

**The system tracks accumulated success, not perfection.**

**Every rung is a mastery lane, including Exposure/Introduction.****  
** **Each lane accumulates success toward a tunable target:**

  - **\*\*Exposure / Introduction: sustained success ≥ 1\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Reproduction / Imitation: sustained success ≥ 2–3\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Comprehension: sustained success ≥ 2–3\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Recognition: sustained success ≥ 3\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Production: sustained success ≥ 2\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Application: sustained success ≥ 1–2\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Transmission: sustained success ≥ 1\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***

**Lane meaning**

  - **\*\*Success at a lane means “confirmed at this lane right now,” not permanent.\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Higher lanes imply stronger evidence than lower lanes.\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***

**Failures**

  - **\*\*Do not wipe progress.\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Pull the next review closer.\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***
  - **\*\*Redirect downward to supports if needed.\*\*\*\*  &#10;  &#10;\*\***

**Energy management**

  - **\*\*Adjust mission size and pacing, not mastery thresholds.\*\***

## **9. Micro-lesson teaching logic (exact)**

**When a concept appears in a mission:**

1.  **\*\*Start at the lane that is due\*\*\*\*  &#10;\*\*\*\*(often production)\*\***
2.  **\*\*If the learner struggles:\*\***  
      - **\*\*drop to recognition\*\***
      - **\*\*then imitate\*\***
      - **\*\*then observe if needed\*\***
3.  **\*\*Accumulate successful interactions\*\***
4.  **\*\*End at the highest successful rung\*\***
5.  **\*\*Update mastery signals for the relevant lane\*\***

**No lesson ends on repeated failure.**

## **10. Spaced repetition schedule (humane default)**

### **Example: Production lane**

  - **\*\*success → +1 day\*\***
  - **\*\*next → +3 days\*\***
  - **\*\*next → +7 days\*\***
  - **\*\*next → +21 days\*\***
  - **\*\*next → +60 days\*\***
  - **\*\*next → +180 days\*\***
  - **\*\*next → +365 days\*\***

**After this:**

  - **\*\*the concept enters maintenance\*\***
  - **\*\*it resurfaces only if:\*\***  
      - **\*\*used as a prerequisite\*\***
      - **\*\*confidence drops\*\***
      - **\*\*errors appear\*\***
      - **\*\*an explicit challenge or review is requested\*\***

**Recognition can advance faster and cap earlier.**

## **11. Application-level evidence and retirement**

### **Core rule (simple, strong)**

**If a learner can correctly apply a concept in context, drills retire.**

**Hierarchy:****  
****Apply \> Produce \> Recognize****  
****Apply is king.**

### **What retirement means**

**Retire does not mean delete or forget.**

**It means:**

  - **\*\*removed from mandatory spaced repetition\*\***
  - **\*\*no longer appears in missions by default\*\***

**Still available:**

  - **\*\*contextually (reading, writing, speaking)\*\***
  - **\*\*optionally (challenge, review, gamification)\*\***
  - **\*\*diagnostically (if errors reappear)\*\***

**Training mode → background competence.**

## **12. Implicit mastery detection**

**Mastery may also be supported by authentic use, such as:**

  - **\*\*writing sentences or compositions\*\***
  - **\*\*speaking naturally\*\***
  - **\*\*solving problems\*\***
  - **\*\*teaching others\*\***

**Rules:**

  - **\*\*Correct application counts as Apply-level evidence\*\***
  - **\*\*Errors are linked back to concept nodes\*\***
  - **\*\*Parent concepts may be reviewed if needed\*\***
  - **\*\*Implicit evidence accelerates mastery but never fully replaces explicit checks\*\***

## **13. Confidence as signal (not proof)**

**Learner self-reports: easy / ok / hard**

**High confidence:**

  - **\*\*reduces frequency\*\***
  - **\*\*may schedule a delayed challenge\*\***

**Confidence never replaces evidence.****  
****Evidence + time = mastery.**

## **14. The unifying rule**

**The curriculum tree defines order, mastery records stability at recognition and production, micro-lessons move up and down the ladder humanely, learner confidence provides pacing signals, authentic application retires drills, and spaced repetition proves what survives time.**
