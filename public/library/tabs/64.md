# **Core Ontological Categories (20-Question Framework)**

This list defines the primary categories of “things that exist” for modeling, classification, and knowledge representation.

# **Human-Scale Ontology v1**

## **I. Living & Natural**

1.  **Living Being****  
    ** (person, dog, tree)  
      
2.  **Natural Object****  
    ** (rock, river, mountain)  
      
3.  **Material / Substance****  
    ** (water, wood, steel, air)  
      

## **II. Human-Made Physical (Artifacts)**

1.  **Artifact (General Man-Made Object)****  
    ** (phone, bowl, zipper)  
      

Subtypes based on human interaction:

1.  **Tool****  
    ** Hand-used object for direct action  
     (hammer, knife, paintbrush)  
      
2.  **Device****  
    ** Engineered object with mechanism  
     (phone, calculator, MRI machine)  
      
3.  **Vehicle****  
    ** Transports people or goods  
     (car, airplane, bicycle)  
      
4.  **Equipment****  
    ** Functional items used in activity/system  
     (gym equipment, lab equipment, safety gear)  
      
5.  **Container****  
    ** Holds or stores things  
     (box, bottle, bowl)  
      
6.  **Structure****  
    ** Built stationary object  
     (house, bridge, tower)  
      
7.  **Infrastructure****  
    ** Large-scale enabling system  
     (roads, power grid, internet network)  
      

## **III. Spatial & Temporal**

1.  **Place / Location****  
    ** (room, city, under the rock)  
      
2.  **Time / Temporal Region****  
    ** (yesterday, January, 3pm)  
      

## **IV. Activity & Change**

1.  **Event****  
    ** Something that happens  
     (wedding, fight, lesson)  
      
2.  **Process****  
    ** Ongoing change  
     (learning, cooking, growing)  
      
3.  **Action****  
    ** Intentional act  
     (writing, throwing, speaking)  
      
4.  **State / Condition****  
    ** Mode of being  
     (hungry, closed, broken)  
      

## **V. Information & Abstract**

1.  **Information Object****  
    ** Encoded content  
     (document, sentence, song)  
      
2.  **Abstract Concept****  
    ** Non-physical idea  
     (number, rule, democracy)  
      
3.  **Role****  
    ** Context-dependent identity  
     (teacher, judge, pilot)  
      
4.  **Relation****  
    ** Connection between things  
     (part-of, parent-of, located-in)  
      
5.  **Quantity / Measurement****  
    ** (five, 2 meters, 3 kg)  
      
6.  **Emotion / Mental State****  
    ** (fear, belief, intention)  
      
7.  **Social Construct****  
    ** Exists by shared agreement  
     (money, marriage, law)  
      

This is:

  - Human-sized  
      
  - Structurally clean  
      
  - Broad enough for almost any conversation  
      
  - Not bloated  
      

This is a solid foundation.
