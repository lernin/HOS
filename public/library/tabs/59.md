Got it, Ashley. Here’s the **clean, frozen Circle–Square map**. This is the “outline of reality” version—no scope creep, just capture.

## **ð CIRCLES — Social Reality (soft edges)**

**What they are:****  
**Human relationships that persist across contexts.

**Core elements**

  - **People** – identity follows the person
  - **Relationships** – parent, friend, teacher, peer
  - **Trust & reputation**
  - **Communication** – talk, gesture, norms
  - **Belonging / affiliation**

**Properties**

  - No clear start/end
  - No single purpose
  - Emotional, contextual
  - Travel with the person

Examples: family, friends, classmates *as people*, informal networks

## **ð¦ SQUARES — Purpose-Driven Reality (hard edges)**

**What they are:****  
**Bounded systems created to accomplish something.

### **1. Intent**

  - **Purpose / Goal**
  - **Success criteria**

### **2. Structure**

  - **Rules**
  - **Roles** (people-in-context)
  - **Permissions / authority**

### **3. Processes**

  - **Tasks**
  - **Workflows**
  - **Rituals / routines**

### **4. Physical & Resource Primitives**

  - **Places** – rooms, buildings
  - **Furniture** – activity scaffolding
  - **Equipment** – machines that operate
  - **Tools** – extensions of humans
  - **Vehicles** – movement between places
  - **Materials** – raw inputs
  - **Ingredients** – recipe-bound materials
  - **Parts** – components of durable objects

### **5. Outputs**

  - **Services**
  - **Goods**
  - **Experiences**
  - **Artifacts**

**Properties**

  - Clear boundaries
  - Defined lifecycle
  - Replaceable
  - Governed

Examples: school, taekwondo gym, bakery, hair salon, movie night

## **ð How They Interact**

  - **People live in circles**
  - **People** ***enter*** **squares**
  - Squares borrow trust from circles
  - Circles outlive squares

## **One-line truth**

**Circles are about who we are.****  
****Squares are about what we’re doing—right now, together.**
