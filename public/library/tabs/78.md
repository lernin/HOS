# Handshake  

  

## **Executive Summary — The Handshake**

## **Presidia’s** ***Handshake*** **turns scanning into a guided, real-world interaction. When two people connect, the app prompts them to speak to each other, not to the AI. Their phones mirror simple prompts—introductions, questions, responses—while the AI quietly listens, transcribes, and supports with optional translation.**

## **The result is live, face-to-face conversation with built-in turn-taking, listening, and repair. Learning emerges naturally: when a child taps for translation or asks for a word, the system detects gaps and adds them to the learner’s track—no drills, no tests. Mixed ability levels work because kids help each other in the moment.**

## **After a short exchange, the system lightly clarifies the relationship (classmate, teacher, friend, family, etc.), rewarding successful interaction—not speed or popularity. Points exist, but they reinforce quality interaction, not metrics.**

## **The Handshake replaces shallow scanning and social metrics with intentional, human connection—social practice instead of social media.**

  
  
  

## **Feature: The Handshake (Guided Live Interaction)**

### **Core idea**

Instead of kids scanning each other just for points, **Presidia turns scanning into a live, face-to-face interaction ritual**.

Two kids “handshake” in the app → a shared, guided conversation begins.

### **How it works**

  - Two people initiate a **handshake****  
      
    **
  - Both phones prompt them with simple, mirrored questions:  
      
      
      - “Introduce yourself”  
          
      - “Ask their name”  
          
      - “Respond to what they said”  
          
  - The kids **talk to each other**, not to the AI  
      
  - The AI listens, transcribes, and quietly guides the flow  
      

No chatbot conversation.  
 The AI is a **conversation conductor**, not a participant.

### **Language support (the secret weapon)**

  - Live transcription of what each kid says  
      
  - Optional **tap-to-see translation** if something is unclear  
      
  - If a child needs a translation, the system learns:  
      
      
      - “This concept wasn’t understood”  
          
      - It gets added naturally to their learning track  
          

Learning emerges *from real interaction*, not drills.

### **Mixed levels? No problem.**

  - Even mismatched kids can help each other:  
      
      
      - “What’s the Korean word for pencil?”  
          
      - Spoken answer is detected and understood  
          
  - Reading + listening + speaking all happen organically  
      

Kids become **learning resources for each other**.

### **Relationship clarification (light, playful)**

After a few prompts, the system asks:

  - “What is your relationship?”  
      
      
      - classmate  
          
      - teacher  
          
      - friend  
          
      - family  
          
      - stranger  
          
  - Answering together earns points  
      

This:

  - encourages talking  
      
  - reduces ambiguity  
      
  - feeds the broader social graph naturally  
      

### **Incentives (but not the point)**

  - Points / digital rewards exist  
      
  - But rewards come from **successful interaction**, not clicks  
      
  - Talking well \> scanning fast  
      

### **Why this is special**

  - Real-time  
      
  - Face-to-face  
      
  - Turn-taking  
      
  - Listening  
      
  - Shared effort  
      
  - AI fades into the background  
      

**This is not social media.****  
** **This is social practice.**

## **Handshake Display Behavior (Motion-Aware)**

**Default State (on tap Handshake):**

  - Opens in **Camera Mode**
  - Assumes phone is facing the user
  - Ready to scan immediately

**Display Intent Detection (Auto Mode):**

  - If device rotates outward (screen turns away from user)
  - Or tilts downward past threshold angle
  - System interprets this as **“intent to display”**
  - QR Code auto-reveals

**Manual Controls:**

  - Toggle options:
      
      - Camera
      - Code
      - Auto (motion-aware)

**Design Principles:**

  - No accidental exposure
  - Code appears only on clear physical intent
  - Gesture mirrors real-world behavior (flip to show)
  - Feels discoverable and “magical”
  - Reduces friction in high-volume environments (conference / classroom)

**Extended Use:**

  - Same handshake gesture used for:
      
      - Social connection
      - Classroom attendance (poster scan)
      - Event check-in
      - Section join
