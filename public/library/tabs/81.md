## **Dunbar Layers & Intimacy Model**

### **Purpose**

This model describes **how close people are**, not just whether they’re connected.  
 It helps the system **pace interactions**, protect attention, and encourage healthy social growth—especially in learning environments.

## **The Four Core Layers**

### **1. Core (≈5)**

**Who they are:**

  - Ride-or-die people
  - Deep trust, emotional safety
  - Daily or near-daily interaction

**What happens here:**

  - Emotional support
  - Honest feedback
  - Vulnerability

**System behavior:**

  - Highest interaction priority
  - Rich, frequent messages
  - No filtering for brevity (only safety)

### **2. Close (≈15)**

**Who they are:**

  - Strong friendships
  - Teammates
  - Trusted collaborators

**What happens here:**

  - Encouragement
  - Learning together
  - Problem-solving

**System behavior:**

  - Regular interaction
  - Encouragement + collaboration prompts
  - High social weight in missions

### **3. Community (≈50)**

**Who they are:**

  - Classmates
  - Club members
  - People you know well enough to work with comfortably

**What happens here:**

  - Civility
  - Cooperation
  - Friendly acknowledgment

**System behavior:**

  - Light encouragement
  - Group participation signals
  - Safe peer interactions

In a **healthy classroom**, this is the **minimum expected layer** for everyone.

### **4. Ambient (≈150)**

**Who they are:**

  - Familiar faces
  - Weak ties
  - “I know who they are”

**What happens here:**

  - Politeness
  - Recognition
  - Minimal emotional exchange

**System behavior:**

  - Very limited messaging
  - No emotional loading
  - Mostly awareness, not engagement

## **Beyond 150**

**Meaning:**

  - Not negative
  - Simply no relationship bandwidth

**System behavior:**

  - No direct interaction by default
  - Only public or system-level signals

## **Key Design Principles**

### **1. 50 Is the Floor in Shared Spaces**

In classrooms, teams, or cohorts:

  - Everyone should ideally be within the **50 layer**
  - This supports respect, safety, and collaboration
  - Falling outside this signals a problem to address, not “normal”

### **2. Intimacy Is Dynamic**

  - Relationships move over time
  - Kindness, collaboration, and shared work increase proximity
  - Neglect or harm decreases it

The system tracks this **implicitly**, not with labels.

### **3. No Forced Friendship**

  - Not everyone becomes a 15 or a 5
  - That’s healthy
  - The goal is **civility and warmth**, not emotional overload

### **4. System Uses Intimacy to Regulate Interaction**

  - Prevents spam and overwhelm
  - Prioritizes voices that matter
  - Protects emotional energy

## **Why This Matters for Learning**

  - People learn better from people they trust
  - Peer encouragement is more powerful than system praise
  - Social reinforcement improves retention and motivation

The Dunbar model ensures:

**Connection without chaos.****  
** **Warmth without overwhelm.****  
** **Growth without coercion.**
