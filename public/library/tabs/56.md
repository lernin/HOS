# Kids must love this  

Ashley — kids will like it **if it feels like it’s on their side**, not testing them.

Here’s what actually makes them lean in:

1.  **It starts with** ***their*** **words****  
    ** Kids love hearing *themselves* turned into something better.  
     “Whoa, that’s my sentence?” → instant ownership.  
      
2.  **They don’t get punished for being wrong****  
    ** Mistakes quietly turn into “help later,” not red marks now.  
     That alone drops anxiety fast.  
      
3.  **It stops before they’re tired****  
    ** The capacity limit is huge.  
     When it ends early, kids feel smart, not drained.  
      
4.  **They get a finished thing they’re proud of****  
    ** Diary entry. Clean page. Read-aloud recording.  
     Kids care way more about *artifacts* than scores.  
      
5.  **They feel smarter each loop****  
    ** Read → say → write → fix → reread.  
     They *feel* progress in their body, not in a chart.  
      
6.  **They get to say “this was easy / I need help” safely****  
    ** That tiny agency moment is addictive.  
     It feels honest, not risky.  
      
7.  **It feels calm, not loud****  
    ** No flashing UI. No spammy rewards.  
     Quiet systems earn trust with kids.  
      

My blunt take:  
 Kids will like this because **it behaves like a patient teacher who notices them**.

Not a game.  
 Not a judge.  
 Not a chatbot showing off.

That’s rare — and kids instantly feel it.
