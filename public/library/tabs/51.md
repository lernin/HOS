### **Authority emergence rule (Phase 0 — FINAL, locked)**

**Website entry (Begin):**

  - No Home Primary is created. A home primary happens when a parent/guardian connects to a student/child in some way.  
      
  - Adults entering independently never become Home Primary.  
      

**Child-anchored entry (code-based onboarding):**

  - If a parent / guardian is created **via a child’s connection code**, and  
      
  - the child’s Home has **no Home Primary**,  
     → **that adult becomes Home Primary immediately.****  
    ** (Not optional. No prompt.)  
      

**Assignment rules:**

  - Home Primary is assigned **once, automatically**, on first child context.  
      
  - Adding more children does **not** change it.  
      
  - Adding more adults does **not** change it.  
      

**Transfer rule (important):**

  - Home Primary **can be explicitly transferred** by the current Home Primary.  
      
  - Transfers are intentional, logged actions (not automatic).  
      
  - There is never more than one Home Primary at a time.  
      

**Global invariants:**

  - No children → no Home Primary.  
      
  - Child context creates authority.  
      
  - Adult intent alone never does.  
      

### **One-line invariant (keep this burned in)**

Home Primary is created by child context, assigned once, and only changes by explicit transfer.

  -   

## **What this enables later**

  - Coordination roles appear only when needed  
      
  - Children/dependents trigger structured roles cleanly  
      
  - Multiple adults can share a Home without power dynamics  
      
  - Adult learning remains private by default  
      
  - Visibility is driven by consent, not titles

### **Core principle**

**Teaching context is private, selectable, and scoped.****  
** Students only ever see *the context they share with you*.

### **How it works (simple + future-proof)**

**1. Active teaching context**

  - A teacher can have multiple schools / contexts  
      
  - **One is “active” at a time****  
      
    **
  - Active context controls:  
      
      
      - Classroom view  
          
      - Sections  
          
      - Grading / assignments  
          
      - What students see  
          

**2. Context switch lives in Profile**

  - Top-right profile avatar (Google-style)  
      
  - Click → “Teaching context”  
      
  - List of schools / tutor contexts  
      
  - Tap to switch (instant, no drama)  
      

**3. Student visibility rule (important)**

  - Students **never see**:  
      
      
      - Other schools you teach at  
          
      - Other roles you hold  
          
  - They only see:  
      
      
      - You-as-their-teacher-in-this-section  
          

This keeps trust clean and avoids confusion.

### **Why this is the right abstraction**

  - Matches real teacher behavior  
      
  - Avoids “why is my teacher in another school?” moments  
      
  - Keeps authority scoped  
      
  - Plays perfectly with capabilities later  
      

My opinion:  
 ✅ Do **active teaching context****  
** ❌ Don’t expose multi-school reality to students  
 ⏳ Defer time-based restrictions
