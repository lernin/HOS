# **PART 1 — SYSTEM OVERVIEW**

## **1.1 Purpose**

The Procedia Linguistic Annotation System is a unified framework for:

1. educational grammar correction  
2. linguistic annotation  
3. AI-readable language analysis  
4. token-level language decomposition  
5. sentence analysis  
6. student mastery tracking  
7. adaptive curriculum generation  
8. machine-assisted language teaching  
9. structured correction tracking  
10. NLP-compatible annotation  
11. linguistic ontology construction  
12. educational analytics

The system functions simultaneously as:

* an educational correction engine  
* a linguistic annotation language  
* a token classification system  
* an NLP-compatible structured representation  
* a machine-learning annotation schema  
* an educational feedback engine

Annotations should remain understandable by:

# **PART 3 — NOUN CATEGORY SYSTEM (THE 9 BLUES)**

| Code | Name | Description | Required Operation | Example |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| N01 | Single noun | One specific thing | determiner | the dog |
| N02 | Plural noun (specific) | Multiple specific things | determiner \+ pluralization | the dogs |
| N03 | All count noun (general) | General category | pluralization | Dogs are friendly |
| N04 | Unique noun | Proper noun | capitalization | Canada |
| N05 | Pronoun | Replacement noun | none | I, she |
| N06 | Representative noun | Single represents category | determiner | A soldier is strong |
| N07 | Measure noun (specific) | Substance with determiner | measure determiner | the milk |
| N08 | Measure noun (general) | General substance | none | I like milk |
| N09 | Gerund noun | Verb acting as noun | \-ing | Running is fun |

---

# **N01 — Single Noun**

## **Definition**

A single identifiable count noun.

## **Requirements**

* singular  
* count determiner

## **Allows**

* the dog  
* a dog  
* my dog

## **Forbids**

* dog  
* many dog  
* much dog

## **Notes**

N01 nouns require a determiner because they represent one identifiable countable object.

---

# **N02 — Plural Noun (Specific)**

## **Definition**

Multiple identifiable count nouns.

## **Requirements**

* count determiner  
* pluralization

## **Allows**

* the dogs  
* many dogs  
* these dogs

## **Forbids**

* the dog  
* many dog  
* dogs (if specificity intended)

## **Notes**

N02 nouns represent specific plural groups.

---

# **N03 — All Count Noun (General)**

## **Definition**

A general count category.

## **Requirements**

* pluralization

## **Allows**

* Dogs are friendly.  
* Tigers are dangerous.

## **Forbids**

* Dogs are friendly.  
* The dogs are friendly. (changes meaning)

## **Notes**

N03 nouns refer to an entire category rather than specific objects.

---

# **N04 — Unique Noun**

## **Definition**

A proper noun representing a unique entity.

## **Requirements**

* capitalization

## **Allows**

* Canada  
* Ashley  
* Seoul

## **Forbids**

* canada  
* ashley  
* seoul

## **Notes**

N04 nouns are grammatically unique and require capitalization.

---

# **N05 — Pronoun**

## **Definition**

A replacement noun.

## **Requirements**

* none

## **Allows**

* I  
* she  
* they

## **Forbids**

* incorrect pronoun forms  
* pronoun case mismatches

## **Notes**

Pronouns replace nouns and carry grammatical features such as:

* person  
* number  
* case

---

# **N06 — Representative Noun**

## **Definition**

A singular noun representing an entire category.

## **Requirements**

* count determiner

## **Allows**

* A tiger is dangerous.  
* A soldier must be brave.

## **Forbids**

* Tiger is dangerous.  
* Soldiers is dangerous.

## **Notes**

N06 uses a single representative member to refer to a general class.

---

# **N07 — Measure Noun (Specific)**

## **Definition**

A measurable substance with specificity.

## **Requirements**

* measure determiner

## **Allows**

* the milk  
* some water  
* much rice

## **Forbids**

* many milk  
* a rice  
* waters

## **Notes**

N07 nouns behave as measurable substances rather than countable units.

---

# **N08 — Measure Noun (General)**

## **Definition**

A general measurable substance.

## **Requirements**

* none

## **Allows**

* I like milk.  
* Water is important.  
* Rice is healthy.

## **Forbids**

* many milk  
* a water  
* waters (unless semantic shift occurs)

## **Notes**

N08 nouns represent substances or materials in a general sense.

---

# **N09 — Gerund Noun**

## **Definition**

A verb functioning as a noun.

## **Requirements**

* \-ing transformation

## **Allows**

* Running is fun.  
* Swimming is healthy.

## **Forbids**

* Runing is fun.  
* Run is fun. (changes structure)

## **Notes**

N09 nouns originate from verbs but function grammatically as nouns.

# **PART 13 — TOKEN FORMAT**

## **13.1 Correct Token**

surface.category.lemma.pos.sense\[inflection\]

Example:

dogs.N03.dog.n.01\[pl\]

---

## **13.2 Error Token**

\[surface.category:ERROR:correction.category\]

Example:

\[drinked.N05:S25:drank.N05\]
