## **Classroom Conversation Capture (Teacher Tool)**

### **Purpose**

The **Classroom Conversation Capture** is a live, teacher-controlled tool that quietly records real teaching moments as they happen in class—without interrupting instruction or pulling attention to screens. It turns natural classroom talk (corrections, explanations, examples, questions) into reusable learning artifacts for students.

This is not a chatbot and not a lesson player.  
It is a **listening engine** that follows the teacher.

### **Core Idea**

The teacher teaches normally.  
The system listens, records, and organizes.

Anything captured during section becomes **shared learning material** that students can later review, claim, and work with in Missions—without extra work from the teacher.

### **How It Works (Teacher Experience)**

  - The teacher sees a **Classroom Control Panel** (the “steering wheel” of the class).
  - Students’ desks are **locked (black screen)** by default to protect attention.
  - While speaking, the teacher can:
      
      - **Hold** on a student or the global trackpad → record a generic teaching note.
      - **Swipe while holding** to bias intent:
          
          - Left → spelling
          - Right → pronunciation
          - Up → grammar / usage
          - Down → meaning / explanation
  - The teacher speaks naturally:
      
      - corrections
      - examples
      - definitions
      - translations
      - explanations
  - No forms. No modes. No labels spoken to the system.

Audio is always the source of truth.

### **What Gets Captured**

Each capture may include:

  - raw audio (always)
  - inferred target word or phrase
  - incorrect vs correct forms (if present)
  - language pair (EN / KO)
  - grammar concepts (e.g. tense, agreement)
  - who triggered it (student), but **public by default**

Holding on a student **tags context**, not ownership.  
Anything said in section can be used by everyone.

### **Grammar & Corrections (Key Strength)**

The system is designed to capture **live error–repair moments**, such as:

  - “You said *I good to the school*, but it’s *I went to the school*.”
  - Partial captures (error only)
  - Full explanations (error + correction + rule)

Corrections can be:

  - immediate
  - delayed (students try first)
  - refined later in advanced review

This mirrors real teaching, not worksheets.

### **Student Experience (After Class)**

Captured items appear **once** at the start of the student’s Mission:

  - “Review what we talked about today”

Students can respond by swiping:

  - Right → *I need to learn this*
  - Down → *I’m confused*
  - Left → *I know this*
  - Up → *This was my question*

All responses are positive and rewarded.  
Items expire if missed, encouraging immediate engagement.

### **Why This Matters**

  - No extra teacher prep
  - No note-taking burden
  - No attention theft during instruction
  - Real section moments become learning content
  - Review happens **outside class**, not during it

This feature turns everyday classroom talk into a structured learning pipeline—without changing how teachers teach.
