# Woodland Walk — asset provenance

Nature models: Quaternius, Stylized Nature MegaKit (free Standard collection), CC0.
https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html
https://poly.pizza/bundle/Stylized-Nature-MegaKit-T34GZFA0fm

Individual source URLs and download URLs are recorded in models.json. GLBs have been repacked with textures resized to at most 512 pixels and foliage materials adjusted for real-time browser rendering. scripts/prepare-woodland-assets.cjs reproduces this step (requires Node, curl, and sharp).

Forest recording: “Forest #3”, Pierre SIBANARCO, BigSoundBank, CC0.
https://bigsoundbank.com/forest-3-s2715.html
https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html
Original: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/2715.mp3
Re-encoded to 96 kbps MP3. This is a recorded soundscape, not a live stream.

Music: original Web Audio synthesis composed for this experiment; spacious four-chord D-major progression, sine-tone envelopes and a generated reverberation impulse. No commercial music recordings, remote AI service, microphone, or speech recognition.
